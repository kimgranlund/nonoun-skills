Scoring tiers

  • ΔE_ok < 0.02 — perfect match (below JND)
  • 0.02 – 0.05 — excellent (close enough that you'd need side-by-side to notice)
  • 0.05 – 0.10 — good (a careful observer would see the difference)
  • 0.10 – 0.20 — visible mismatch
  • > 0.20 — obviously different colors
