How the histograms are weighted

The hue ring bins pixels by OKLCh hue (5° per bin) but weights each pixel's contribution by its chroma. Near-gray pixels contribute almost nothing — the ring shows the image's actual dominant colorful hues, not just where most of its pixels happen to fall in hue space.

The lightness histogram counts pixels per L bin (32 bins). Each bar is rendered at that bin's neutral encoded sRGB value, so a "midnight" image shows a dark histogram and a snowy image shows a bright one.
