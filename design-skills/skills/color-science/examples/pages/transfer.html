Transfer curves

The non-linearities that map linear light (radiometrically additive) to display-encoded signal (perceptually distributed). Linear input on X, encoded output on Y. The dashed diagonal is the identity reference.

Why this matters

Every gamut-specific RGB space pairs a set of primaries with a transfer function. Mixing them up — applying encoded values where linear is expected (or vice versa) — produces wrong colors that are surprisingly hard to debug.

sRGB and Display P3 share the IEC 61966-2-1 piecewise transfer (linear segment near zero, then 1.055 · x1/2.4 − 0.055). Rec.709 and Rec.2020 share the BT.2020 OETF. Adobe RGB uses pure 2.2 gamma. ProPhoto uses a piecewise 1.8 (slope 16 near zero, then x1/1.8). PQ and HLG are the two BT.2100 HDR transfers.
