Color-blindness simulator

Drop an image to see how it looks under each type of color vision deficiency. Severity is continuously adjustable — slide from full dichromacy (no cone signal) down to mild anomalous trichromacy. Simulation uses Machado 2009 matrices on linear sRGB (the same algorithm Chrome DevTools uses).

Why this matters

About 8% of men and 0.4% of women have some form of red-green color vision deficiency. Protanopia and deuteranopia are by far the most common; tritanopia is rare (under 1 in 10,000). Designs that rely on red/green to convey state (the classic "green = good, red = bad") look identical to a deuteranope — they need a second cue.

The Machado matrices are physiologically grounded — derived from cone spectral sensitivity data — and parameterized by severity. At severity 0 they're the identity (no transform); at 1.0 they're full dichromacy. Intermediate values approximate anomalous trichromacy.
