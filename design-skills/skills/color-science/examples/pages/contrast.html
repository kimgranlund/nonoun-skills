Why APCA over WCAG 2.x

WCAG 2.x has documented limitations: it's polarity-blind (white-on-black and black-on-white score identically, but the eye sees them differently), spatial-frequency-blind (no font-size or stroke-weight awareness), and produces mid-range distortion (it passes pairs that look poor, rejects pairs that look fine).

APCA fixes all three by using polarity-sensitive lightness contrast and a calibrated readability scale. Pass WCAG 2.2 AA for legal compliance; design to APCA for actual readability.

APCA tiers (Bronze Simple Mode)

  • |Lc| ≥ 90 — Optimal: sustained body reading
  • 75–89 — Fluent body (recommended baseline)
  • 60–74 — Body minimum readable
  • 45–59 — Headings ≥24pt or large body ≥18pt bold
  • 30–44 — Large headings ≥36pt
  • 15–29 — Non-text only (icons, decorative)
  • <15 — Insufficient
