Gamut envelope

At a fixed OKLCh hue, the gamut of each RGB space is a 2D region in (L, C). The wider the gamut, the higher the achievable chroma. Each region's cusp — its widest point — is computed via Ottosson's closed-form solver. Click anywhere on the chart to read out an OKLCh value.

What's plotted

The X axis is OKLCh chroma (0 to 0.4). The Y axis is OKLCh lightness (0 to 1). For each (L, C) at the current hue, we check whether the resulting color lies within each gamut's [0, 1]³ linear-RGB cube. The widest-supported gamut "wins" in the rendering, so you see sRGB as the inner region and Rec.2020 as the outermost.

The black dot marks the sRGB cusp — the maximum-chroma point at this hue, computed via Ottosson's three-face polynomial + Halley refinement (closed-form, ~6 floating-point operations per call).
