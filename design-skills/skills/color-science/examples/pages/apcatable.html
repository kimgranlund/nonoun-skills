How to read the table

The official APCA Bronze Simple Mode publishes minimum Lc values per (font size × weight) bucket. Body-text combinations need Lc ≥ 75 for fluent reading. Large headers can pass with Lc ≥ 45. Non-text icons can pass with Lc ≥ 15.

A "PASS" cell here means: for that font size and weight, |Lc| of the current foreground/background pair meets or exceeds the published threshold. Cells in green are safe, cells in orange are borderline (within 10 Lc of pass), and cells in red fail.
