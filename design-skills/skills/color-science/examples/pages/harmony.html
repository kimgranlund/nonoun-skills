Why two rows?

Both rows use the same hue angles. In OKLCh they're perceptual angles — 180° actually means visually opposite. In HSL the angles live on a deformed cylinder: "120°" is yellow-green-on-blue territory, "180°" can produce a complement that looks dimmer or brighter than the base. Designers often ship HSL harmonies and then hand-correct each swatch's lightness. OKLCh removes that step.
