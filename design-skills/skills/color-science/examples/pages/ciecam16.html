Comparison to defaults

The display-default condition is La ≈ 64, Yb at L*=50, average surround — the row-1 numbers above match HCT. As you change the controls, the J/M/h values drift relative to that baseline. Try setting surround to Dark: J drops (the eye adjusts), M shifts — chromatic content appears differently in cinema-style viewing.

Caveat: this is an appearance model, not a reproduction model

J/M/h answer the question "what does this stimulus look like under these conditions?" not "what color do I display to make it look like this?" For the reproduction side, you'd want CAM16-UCS (a perceptually-uniform Cartesian rearrangement of CIECAM16) or Material's HCT (which fixes h to be a perceptual angle).
