CVD-safe palette designer

Greedy hue optimization maximizes the worst-case ΔE_ok across all 4 viewing conditions. The colors below are all at the same lightness, so the only thing distinguishing them is hue (which is what CVD compresses).

Why "worst case across CVD types"?

A palette can look fine under normal vision and totally collapse under deuteranopia — the most common CVD (about 6% of men). If you optimize only for normal-vision contrast, you're leaving the largest accessibility population behind.

The reverse trap: optimizing only for protan/deutan can collapse separation on tritanopia (rare but real). The right objective is "worst-case across all viewing conditions" — guarantee a minimum separation under the union.

This demo uses Machado-2009 simulation at full severity (severity 1.0 = full dichromacy). The optimization is greedy coordinate-descent on hue angles. Better results would use:

  • Multiple severity levels (0.5, 1.0) for anomalous trichromacy
  • Pair-wise lightness adjustment, not just hue
  • A non-greedy global optimizer (simulated annealing, etc.)
