Why two metrics?

"Closest" depends on what space you measure in. RGB Euclidean is perceptually uneven — the same byte distance feels bigger in greens than in blues. ΔE_ok measures distance in OKLab, where equal numbers correspond to equal perceived differences. For "what would a human call this color?", ΔE_ok is the right answer.

The CSS named color list (the 147 names from the X11 / SVG palette shipped with every browser) is what's compared. For more naming coverage, you'd use a wider dictionary like the XKCD color-naming survey results (954 names) or ISCC-NBS centroids (267 names) — both available in references/techniques/color-name-lists.md.
