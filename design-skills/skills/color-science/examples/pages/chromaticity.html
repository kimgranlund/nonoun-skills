CIE 1931 xy chromaticity

The horseshoe is the spectral locus — every monochromatic wavelength between 380 and 700 nm plotted in (x, y). Inside is every color a human can see. The triangles are RGB gamuts: each is the convex hull of three primaries. The wider the triangle, the more saturated the colors that gamut can encode.

Notes

The diagram is intentionally not perceptually uniform — equal visual differences are not equal distances. MacAdam's 1942 ellipses sketched this distortion (a deep-blue ellipse is hundreds of times smaller than a green ellipse at the same scale). Use OKLab or CAM16-UCS when uniformity matters.

White points: D65 is the canonical web/sRGB white, D50 is the ICC profile-connection-space white, D55 is photographic daylight, A is tungsten incandescent.
