Procedural palettes

Two classic procedural color formulas: Cubehelix (D. A. Green 2011 — perceptually monotonic in luminance, designed for science visualization) and iq cosine (Inigo Quilez — a + b · cos(2π · (c · t + d)) per channel, the workhorse of generative art).

Cubehelix params

180 -1.5 1.0 1.0

iq cosine params (per RGB channel)

a — offset

b — amplitude

c — frequency

d — phase

Why luminance plot?

A good colormap for data has monotonically increasing perceived luminance — so that grayscale fallback preserves ordering and colorblind viewers can still read it. Cubehelix is designed specifically for this; iq cosine palettes typically aren't. Watch the Y curve while you slide.
