Why peakC matters

The shape of the sRGB gamut at fixed hue isn't a rectangle in (L, C) space — it's a teardrop with peak chroma near the cusp. A naive "give me 11 colors at hue X and chroma 0.18" request will produce stops that float outside the gamut at the bright and dark ends.

Bounding by peakC(L, hue, gamut) — Ottosson's binary search against the linear-RGB cube — guarantees each stop is reachable, while maximizing chroma at each lightness for the most vivid possible ramp.
